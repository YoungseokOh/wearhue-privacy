Delete your WearHue account

WearHue · developer ZEROSTONZ

In the app

Open Settings → Delete account and photos → Delete. This removes your private wardrobe, uploaded photos, generated results and remaining credits, and deletes the Firebase sign-in identity. It does not delete your Google account.

Without the app

Email seok7302@gmail.com with the subject “WearHue account deletion” from the email used to sign in. We may ask you to verify ownership. Do not send your password, purchase token or payment details.

What remains

Minimal account tombstones, purchase ownership, token hashes and transaction records remain to prevent replay and reconcile refunds. Deleted cloud objects can remain recoverable under the storage provider’s seven-day soft-delete retention. Provider-held OpenAI records follow its separate controls. Saved images on your device remain until you delete them.
