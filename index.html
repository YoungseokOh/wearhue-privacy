WearHue Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

WearHue processes photos locally to preview clothing colors. The current release does not create accounts, display ads, process payments, connect to cloud AI, or send analytics to an external service.

Photos and generated images

  • The Android system picker provides only the photo selected by the user.
  • Camera capture uses an installed camera app and an app-private temporary file.
  • Photos, masks, previews, and generated images remain on the device.
  • A result is added to the media library only after the user selects Save and WearHue verifies the file can be decoded.
  • WearHue does not upload photos or generated images.

Data collection and sharing

WearHue does not collect or share personal data in the current release. It has no account system, advertising SDK, external analytics provider, or Internet permission.

Retention and deletion

App-private cache files may remain while an edit is active. Android or WearHue may clear them. Users can remove app-private data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app, and can delete exports with their gallery or file manager.

Children

WearHue is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

If a future version introduces cloud AI, advertising, payments, accounts, or external analytics, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before distribution.
